419-460-6884 ↗
What if I’ve never painted before?+

You’re welcome here. Nate’s classes are beginner-friendly, with guidance throughout. No previous painting experience is needed.

Do I need to bring supplies?+

All painting materials are included in Nate’s workshops. Bring your curiosity and wear something you’re comfortable painting in.

How much does a class cost?+

The featured painting workshops with Nate are $60 per person, including materials. Guest workshops may have different prices; check the studio’s current listing.

How do I get a place in a class?+

Classes are first come, first served. Check the official class schedule, then call 419-460-6884 to ask about availability.

Is there more than landscape painting?+

The studio also hosts guest instructors. Explore Bob Ross & Friends for other workshops, including acrylic pouring. Interested in teaching? Get in touch with the studio.
